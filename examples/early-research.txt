Early Research: Team Dashboard Filters

Purpose

We are exploring whether operations managers have a recurring problem with rebuilding the same dashboard filters.

Research so far

We spoke with 5 operations managers.

- 3 said they recreate the same filter combination several times each week.
- 1 said they usually start from the same filters but adjust them depending on the task.
- 1 said they rarely reuse the same filters and does not see this as a meaningful problem.

Current observations

Users who repeatedly monitor the same subset of accounts appear to experience the most friction.

The evidence is mixed, so we do not yet know whether this is a broad need or limited to a specific workflow.

Current conclusion

There is enough signal to continue discovery, but not enough evidence to define a solution.

Next step

Interview 5 more operations managers, particularly users who monitor fixed groups of accounts, and understand:

- how frequently they rebuild filters;
- whether their filter combinations remain stable;
- how much time or frustration this creates;
- whether existing workarounds are sufficient.
