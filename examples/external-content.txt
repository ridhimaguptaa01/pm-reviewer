Draft Article: The Cost of Manual Recovery

Businesses often underestimate the operational cost of recovering lost records manually.

To illustrate the impact, consider three scenarios.

Assumptions

- Labor cost: $100 per hour.
- Scenario A requires 10 hours of manual recovery.
- Scenario B requires 20 hours.
- Scenario C requires 100 hours.
- Product recovery cost is $20.

Manual recovery cost

Scenario A:
10 hours × $100 = $1,000

Scenario B:
20 hours × $100 = $2,000

Scenario C:
100 hours × $100 = $10,000

Conclusion

Across all three scenarios, manual recovery costs approximately $3,000.

By comparison, the product restores every dataset instantly with zero manual intervention for only $20.

This means businesses can eliminate recovery effort entirely while reducing recovery costs by more than 99%.

For teams managing multiple customer environments, the savings become even more significant because the same $20 recovery cost applies regardless of data volume.
